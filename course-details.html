Pet Care Essentials

A six-month course designed to introduce students to responsible pet care and animal wellbeing.

About This Course

Pet Care Essentials helps students understand the everyday needs of pets. Students learn about nutrition, hygiene, health, safety and responsible animal care.

Course duration: 6 Months

Course type: Professional Training

Learning approach: Theory and practical activities

Calculate Course Fees

What You Will Learn

The course is divided into three learning stages to help students build their knowledge gradually.

Months 1–2: Introduction to Pet Care

  • Understanding different pets
  • Basic animal needs
  • Responsible pet ownership
  • Daily care routines

Months 3–4: Health and Wellbeing

  • Pet nutrition and feeding
  • Animal hygiene
  • Recognising health concerns
  • Pet safety and prevention

Months 5–6: Practical Pet Care

  • Practical care activities
  • Handling animals safely
  • Applying pet care knowledge
  • Course review and assessment
