ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

Who We Are

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a pet care education and animal training academy based in Durban. We aim to improve animal wellbeing by providing useful knowledge and practical training.

Our academy offers learning opportunities for pet owners, families, pet sitters, kennel attendants, veterinary assistants and individuals interested in building a future in the pet care industry.

We believe that every animal deserves proper care, understanding and attention. Through education, we help people become more confident and responsible when caring for animals.
